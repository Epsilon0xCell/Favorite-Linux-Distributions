Hybrid plate, below the two specimens

My pick, a combination, not a product

Ultimate OS: Garuda Linux Cosmic Desktop

That heading is my own line from the README. I mean Garuda, but with Cosmic instead of KDE. I state it plainly here: this hybrid is my preferred combination. It is not a distribution anyone can download as such, and it is not a release.

From Plate 2: Cosmic desktop

I take the Cosmic desktop instead of KDE. That is the desktop side of the pairing I want.
